## Feature Details

### AI Training Plan Generation
The AI builds a personalised training program based on:
- **Goal:** Muscle gain, fat loss, strength, or endurance
- **Experience level:** Beginner, intermediate, or advanced
- **Days per week:** 2–6 days
- **Program type:** Push/Pull/Legs, Full Body, or Upper/Lower (AI selects the best fit)

Output: A complete week-by-week program with exercises, sets, reps, and target weights. Includes coach notes explaining the programming rationale. Regenerate at any time.

---

### Workout Logging
Log every set as you go — weight, reps, and completion. The app:
- Tracks session duration
- Detects and flags personal records (PRs) automatically
- Calculates estimated 1RM using the Epley formula
- Generates a session summary card (shareable)

Free users: 3 sessions/month cap. Pro users: unlimited.

---

### Exercise Library
A searchable database of exercises across all muscle groups and equipment types: barbell, dumbbell, cable, machine, bodyweight.

- **Free:** 50 exercises (major compounds)
- **Pro:** 500+ exercises including all isolation and machine movements

---

### DEXA Scan Parsing (Pro)
Upload a DEXA scan PDF. The AI reads it and extracts:
- Body fat percentage
- Lean mass (lbs/kg)
- Visceral fat level (rating)

Results are saved to your body composition history and plotted over time on the Progress page. Supports scans from DexaFit, BodySpec, and most clinical formats.

---

### AI Check-in Coaching (Pro)
A structured weekly coaching session. You log:
- Current body weight
- Energy level this week (1–5)
- Mood and motivation (1–5)
- How well you stuck to your plan (1–5)
- Any notes

The AI reads your recent session history, compares it to your plan, and writes a personalised response: what's going well, what to watch, and one specific coaching cue for the week ahead.

---

### AI Nutrition Protocol (Pro)
Tell the AI:
- Nutrition goal: bulk (surplus), cut (deficit), or maintain
- Dietary restrictions: omnivore, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free
- Target daily calories (or let it calculate)

Output: A full 7-day meal plan with per-meal breakdowns and daily macro targets (protein, carbs, fat, calories). Regenerate at any time for variety.

---

### Arsenal AI Recommendations (Pro)
The AI reviews your training history and current plan and surfaces exercise and equipment recommendations — specifically movements you should add, progress on, or rotate in based on your goal and weak points.
